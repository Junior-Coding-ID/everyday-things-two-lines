A two-line explanation for a 10-year-old: why a metal spoon feels colder than a wooden one, even at the same room temperature — told through the analogy of two supermarket checkout belts running at different speeds, with an honest second line about what the analogy maps and what it leaves out.

## Entry

> A metal spoon feels colder than a wooden one even when both are at room temperature because it carries heat away from your fingers like the faster of two checkout belts.
>
> The groceries stand for heat entering each spoon and the belt speeds for how fast heat spreads through them, but the spoons themselves stay still.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT/entry.txt` (default `/output`), with a self-check that the entry stays inside the 1–400 character gate and is exactly two non-empty lines.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT=/tmp/out node main.ts
```
